TORN Bank Management

Official information and legal documents for the TBM Discord application.

TORN Bank Management provides Discord-based account linking and virtual banking workflows for players of the online game TORN, including bank records, deposit and withdrawal requests, loan requests, announcements and support.

TBM is an independent community application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by TORN or Discord.
